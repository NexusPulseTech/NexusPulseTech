# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security issue in a NexusPulse repository, or in a project NexusPulse maintains, please **do not open a public issue**.

Report it privately instead:

1. Open the **Security** tab of the affected repository.
2. Click **Report a vulnerability** and describe the issue.

If the affected repository belongs to one of our clients, report it to that repository's owner in the same way. We will work with them on the fix.

## What to include

- The affected repository, URL or component
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The expected impact, for example data exposure or privilege escalation
- Your contact details, if you want us to follow up

## What to expect

- We aim to acknowledge your report within **5 business days**.
- We will keep you updated while we investigate and fix the issue.
- Please give us reasonable time to release a fix before you disclose the issue publicly.

Thank you for helping keep NexusPulse and our clients safe.
