NexusPulse builds web platforms, Web3 products and business automation for startups and growing companies. We work on client projects as an outsourced engineering team, and we build our own products.

## What we do

- **Product development:** web apps, multi-tenant SaaS, APIs and admin portals, from MVP to launch.
- **Web3 platforms:** loyalty and quest products, wallet sign-in (SIWE), social and on-chain verification.
- **Automation & AI:** n8n and Make.com workflows, chatbots for Messenger, Zalo OA and websites, custom AI agents.
- **Growth & web presence:** landing pages, company websites, SEO and conversion optimization.

## How we work

- You own the code and the repository.
- Weekly demos, and every change goes through a reviewed pull request.
- Limited access, removed when the project ends.
- NDA by default, with a direct line to the founder.

## Is NexusPulse a fit?

- **Great fit:** startups and SMEs building an MVP, SaaS, Web3 product, automation or a new website.
- **Not a fit:** core banking and other heavily regulated financial systems.
